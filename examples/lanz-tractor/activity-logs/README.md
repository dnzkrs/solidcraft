# Lanz mini tractor in SolidCraft: activity logs

16 activities, written after the work, in the order it happened.

| # | Activity | Date | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | [Inspect the Lanz drawing set](01-inspect-the-drawing-set.md) | 2026-10-25 | Twelve uploaded A3 drawing sheets of a model Lanz tractor were identified sheet by sheet, the copyright and SolidWorks origin were flagged, and the scope was agreed: single parts, modelled in SolidCraft, under `examples/` of the owner's private fork |
| 02 | [Build the modelling pipeline and the first parts](02-modelling-pipeline-and-first-parts.md) | 2026-10-25 | A parametric generator that writes SolidCraft scripts and checks every part against a hand-computed volume was built, the first five motor parts (1-01 to 1-04, 1-09) were modelled with it, and a SolidCraft bug that makes blind holes 3.42 mm short was found and worked around |
| 03 | [Model the crankshaft, flywheel, piston and valve gear](03-crankshaft-flywheel-piston-valve-gear.md) | 2026-10-29 | Eleven moving motor parts (1-14 to 1-31) were modelled, the generator gained multi-body parts and a self-updating parts table, undimensioned sizes were measured from the PDF's own vector lines, and the hand-volume check caught a wrong formula in the checker itself |
| 04 | [Finish the sheet-03 motor parts](04-rest-of-sheet-03.md) | 2026-10-29 | The remaining nineteen motor parts on sheet 03 (oiler, big end, slide valves, valve holder, cam follower, exhaust bracket) were modelled, part names were corrected against the parts list, and a SolidCraft limit with tangent faces was found and avoided |
| 05 | [Model the exhaust stack parts](05-exhaust-parts.md) | 2026-10-29 | The four turned parts of the exhaust stack on sheet 04 (1-36 to 1-39) were read from the drawing's vector lines and modelled, and the readings were confirmed by how the parts fit each other |
| 06 | [Model the accelerator linkage and steering bracket](06-accelerator-linkage-and-steering-bracket.md) | 2026-10-29 | Eleven parts from sheet 04 (1-43 to 1-53) were modelled, using SolidCraft's sheet-metal tools for the folded bracket and its coil command for a spring, and a second SolidCraft bug (hidden sketches reappear after reopening) was found |
| 07 | [Model the flame guards and the valve spring](07-flame-guards-and-valve-spring.md) | 2026-10-29 | The two perforated flame protectors (1-40, 1-41), the front flame guard (1-42) and the wire valve spring (1-34) were modelled, completing the motor group 1-01 to 1-53, after several construction approaches failed and a third SolidCraft bug (an inside-out pipe) was found |
| 08 | [Model the chassis front end, steering brackets and fuel tank](08-chassis-front-end-and-fuel-tank.md) | 2026-10-29 | Fourteen group 2 parts from sheet 04 (2-01 and 2-03 to 2-17) were modelled and checked, including a sheet-metal bracket and a fuel tank with its fittings. A wrongly placed flank on 2-03 passed the volume check but was caught in a render |
| 09 | [Model the axle yoke parts, pivot axle and handle collar](09-yoke-arms-bearing-and-pivot-axle.md) | 2026-10-29 | The last eight parts on sheet 04 (2-13 and 2-18 to 2-24) were modelled from the drawing and its vector lines. One needed a 90° twisted neck, built as a loft that starts inside the plate so the boolean join stays exact |
| 10 | [Model the front wheels, steering arm and lay shaft parts](10-front-wheels-and-lay-shaft.md) | 2026-10-29 | Fourteen parts from sheet 05 (2-25 to 2-37) were modelled, the rim and hub cap profiles taken line by line from their sections. A counterbore pattern placed on a revolved part's seam needed a separation retry and came out 46 mm³ short, until the pattern was turned 45° |
| 11 | [Model the tie rod ends, steering column, steering wheel and lights](11-tie-rod-ends-steering-column-and-lights.md) | 2026-10-29 | Nine parts from sheet 05 (2-38 to 2-46) were modelled. On the way, four more SolidCraft limits were found and worked around: an inside-out revolve, a tangential wire join, a contact-work limit on coils and a hole tangent to a tube wall |
| 12 | [Model the motor covers](12-motor-covers.md) | 2026-10-29 | The three 0.5 mm motor covers (2-47 to 2-49) were modelled after plain extrude cuts on the large thin brass sheets went wrong without a warning. The tray was hollowed with `solid.shell`, and the U-shaped side cover was built with the sheet-metal flange and folds |
| 13 | [Model the rear chassis, housings, rear wheel and seat](13-rear-chassis-housings-wheels-and-seat.md) | 2026-10-29 | Twenty-seven group 3 parts from sheets 05 and 06 (3-01 to 3-26A) were modelled, most section profiles read from the drawing's vector lines. A tapped hole ending in the plane of a distant face came out 2.5 mm too deep after a SolidCraft separation retry |
| 14 | [Model the hand brake, controls and stop light](14-hand-brake-and-controls.md) | 2026-10-29 | Twenty group 3 parts from sheet 07 were modelled: the hand brake parts, the reversing and coupling handles, the shaft adjuster and spacer, the accelerator bolt and link, and the stop light. Two SolidCraft problems came up: a cut of two picked sketch regions removed the wrong amount, and a strap's tongue could not be joined without a retry |
| 15 | [Model the gearbox, coupling and belts](15-gearbox-and-belts.md) | 2026-10-29 | Twenty-eight group 4 parts and the 5-01 belt were modelled from sheets 07, 08 and 11. The gears were built prisms first, because SolidCraft could not join a tooth prism to a round hub it touched. The volume check caught a V-belt whose tangent points were on the wrong side |
| 16 | [Model the mudguards and close the set](16-mudguards-and-closing-the-set.md) | 2026-10-29 | The last modelled parts were added: the hand brake and release handles (3-27B, 3-27E), the seat back rest 3-25D and the two mudguards 3-23/3-24. The mudguards were built already folded from overlapping solids. Four parts the drawings do not define fully were left out by the owner's decision, closing the set at 182 of 186 listed parts |
